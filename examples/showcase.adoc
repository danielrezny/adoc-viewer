== Inline formatting

You can write *bold*, _italic_, `monospace`, *_bold italic_*, #highlighted#
and [.underline]#underlined# or [.line-through]#struck# text.
Unconstrained formatting works mid-word: **Ex**ample and __un__believable.
Chemistry needs subscripts like H~2~O, and math needs superscripts like E=mc^2^.

Typographic replacements: (C) (R) (TM), an em dash -- like this, an ellipsis...
and arrows -> <- => <=. Smart "`double`" and '`single`' quotes, and it's an apostrophe.

Links: {url-asciidoc}[AsciiDoc home], a bare URL https://github.com/asciidoctor,
an email ada@example.com, and a cross reference to <<_lists>> or <<tables,the tables section>>.

Keyboard: press kbd:[Ctrl+Shift+P], click btn:[Save], go to menu:File[Export > PDF].

Hard line breaks +
keep this on its own line.
